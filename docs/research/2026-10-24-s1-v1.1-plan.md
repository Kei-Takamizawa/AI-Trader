# Research plan: S1 version 1.1 (declared before any run)

Date: 2026-10-24. Author: Claude (designer). Status: **declared**. The changes, variants and
decision rules below are fixed before any v1.1 result exists. A change after the first run counts
as a new variant and is reported as such.

Background: the P2 report (`docs/research/2026-10-24-p2-walkforward.md`) found S1 1.0.0 positive
but weak: CAGR 1.13%, max drawdown 6.1%, Sharpe 0.30, return/drawdown 0.18 against B0's 0.40. Its
diagnosis (§4) names low exposure by construction as the first cause: three positions sized at
0.5% risk per trade with 3 × ATR(20) stops hold only about 30–50% of equity, and the rest sits in
cash that earns nothing.

## 1. Changes under test

Both changes act on the diagnosed cause (idle capital). Neither changes the signal.

| ID | Change | Why, before seeing results | What it cannot fix |
|---|---|---|---|
| C1 | **Cash proxy:** each empty slot (top N minus the ETFs that qualify) is held in **SHY** (1–3 year Treasuries, already in U0 and in our data) instead of cash. SHY is then removed from the momentum ranking, so it is never counted twice. No trend filter applies to the proxy. | Document 11 §3 specifies "hold the cash proxy or cash" for S1. 1.0.0 used cash only. Short Treasuries earned roughly the T-bill rate in 2020–2024 with small drawdowns. | It adds a little yield but no edge. It may lose in rate-rise periods (2022). |
| C2 | **Risk per trade 1.0%** instead of 0.5%. 1.0% is the hard cap (`hard_caps.py`); the base configuration stays 0.5%. **Research runs only; needs the owner's approval.** | Doubling the risk budget roughly doubles position sizes until the 25% position cap binds, which raises exposure towards the 75% that three slots allow. | Drawdowns scale up too. It does not improve the per-trade edge; profit factors below 1 stay below 1. |

Not in v1.1: volatility-targeted sizing (it would replace the risk engine's stop-based sizing, a
larger design change), new signals, and a changed universe.

## 2. Variants

There are four families: 1.0.0 (baseline, re-run), C1, C2, and C1+C2. Each family uses the same
pre-declared grid as 1.0.0 (top N ∈ {3, 2} × skip ∈ {0, 5}), selected on training Sharpe per fold,
as in TASK-007b.

- New variants: 3 families × 4 grid points = **12**. The baseline re-run repeats existing
  variants and is not counted again.
- Variants tried before: 5 (S1 4, S2 1). **Total after v1.1: 17.**
- The protocol does not change: 36/6-month walk-forward from 2017-01-03 on the same data
  snapshot (raw `f9469b49…5a5815`, adjusted `8985aaec…5a5c52`, or the 2026-10-23 refresh, as
  recorded in the manifest), with the hold-out from 2024-10-17 still locked.

## 3. Decision rules (fixed now)

A family **passes** only if every rule holds on its chained test windows:

1. Return / max drawdown ≥ **0.40** (B0's level in the P2 report).
2. Max drawdown ≤ **10%** (the owner's limit, Q12).
3. Sharpe (rf = 0) ≥ **0.50**, and deflated Sharpe ratio ≥ **0.80** with N = 17 trials.
4. Positive test return in at least **6 of 9** folds, and a profit factor above 1 in at least
   **5 of 9** folds.
5. Stability: the selected grid point keeps the same sign of test return as its neighbours in at
   least 6 of 9 folds (no knife edge, document 12 §3).

Outcomes:

- **No family passes:** the S1 line stops at 1.0.0 (still the reference strategy for paper
  infrastructure). The next research step is a new idea with its own declared plan, not more
  tuning of S1.
- **One or more families pass:** the passing family with the highest return/drawdown becomes the
  S1 1.1 candidate. It runs in PAPER (P3). The hold-out is opened **once**, only after the owner
  agrees, with the number of variants tried (17) stated in the report.
- C2 needs the owner's approval to run, and to be used anywhere outside research. Even if C2
  passes, raising the configured risk per trade for PAPER or LIVE is a separate owner decision.

## 4. What the implementation task must add (TASK-012)

- A `cash_proxy` parameter for S1 (C1).
- A research-only risk-per-trade override on `aitrader walkforward`, at most the hard cap and
  recorded in the manifest (C2).
- Deflated Sharpe ratio (document 12 §6) and B0's Sharpe in the walk-forward output.
- Per-fold profit factor and exposure in the walk-forward summary (already computed per run).

## 5. Reporting

The report gives every family's results, including the ones that fail. For each family it lists
the variants tried, the deflated Sharpe, exposure, cost share and the per-fold table. Monthly
1–2% remains a reference target, not a pass rule. It is reported as the share of months at ≥ 1%
and ≥ 2%.
