## 5. Diagnosis

- **C1 (SHY cash proxy) has almost no effect.** It changes 3 of 9 folds and the chained result
  by less than 0.1 point. Exposure stays near 20%. The P2 hypothesis that idle cash was the main
  drag is not supported: on the test folds, empty slots were rare or short, and SHY added little when held (not
  measured separately here).
- **C2 (risk 1.0%) scales risk, not quality.** Exposure doubles (about 20% → 40%), return rises
  from 5.2% to 8.7%, and max drawdown doubles (6.1% → 12.5%, above the 10% limit). Sharpe and the
  deflated Sharpe fall slightly. This is what leverage on a strategy with no edge looks like.
- **The weakness is the signal, not the sizing.** Profit factor is below 1 in most folds for
  every family, and the deflated Sharpe is well below 0.5, so the observed Sharpe is consistent
  with selection luck among 17 trials.
- Gaps found in the tooling (they do not change the verdict): rule 5 neighbour test returns and
  the cost share (plan §5) are not in the walk-forward output.

## 6. Next steps

2. The risk per trade stays at 0.5% in configuration. C2 is not proposed for PAPER or LIVE.
3. A new strategy idea needs its own declared plan (document 12). Before that plan, add the
